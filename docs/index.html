Method
  • Sources: Google Search, Reddit, X and LinkedIn via Apify actors, once a week. AI Overview checks via the Google Search scraper on a fixed prompt list; Gemini monthly.
  • Brand mentions: texts matching the brand pattern. Dropped: job ads and unrelated homonyms. Tagged, not dropped: own posts and employees, spam, directory and partner listings.
  • Negative: keyword rules (outage, broken, price increase, refund…). Repeatable rules, not an AI tone score.
  • Industries, narratives and pain clusters: keyword rules, editable in config/sources.json.
  • Pain threads: brand-free queries around the problems the product solves, plus competitor names.
  • AI share: prompts where the brand is named ÷ prompts that got an answer.
  • Signals: negative third-party mention; spam; 2+ negative reviews in 30 days; negative reviews without a reply in 90 days; review spike; AI share down 10+ pp; a pain cluster up 1.5× to 3+ threads.
